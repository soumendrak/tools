Behind the number

What does BMI tell me?
BMI is a screening measure based on height and weight, not a diagnosis or a direct measure of body fat. It does not distinguish muscle from fat. A clinician can interpret it alongside your health history and other measurements. This calculator uses adult categories for ages 20 and older; children and teens need BMI-for-age assessment.
How is it calculated?
Weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared.BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)²Imperial measurements are converted using 1 inch = 2.54 cm and 1 pound = 0.45359237 kg before calculating. This tool accepts heights from 100 to 250 cm and weights from 20 to 350 kg, or their imperial equivalents.
What is the reference weight range?
It is the weight interval that corresponds to a BMI of 18.5 up to, but not including, 25 at your height. It is a mathematical reference, not a personal weight goal. The lower displayed endpoint is rounded up and the upper endpoint down to the nearest 0.1 unit so the displayed values stay inside the interval.

Source: CDC adult BMI categories and BMI limitations.
