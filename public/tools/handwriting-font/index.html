Handwriting Font Maker

Print a template, write, snap a photo — download a TTF of your own handwriting. Everything runs in your browser; photos never leave your device.

1Print the template

Print the sheet (any printer scale works) and fill the cells with a dark pen — the light guides are ignored automatically. Sit letters on the dashed baseline; let descenders (g, y, p) hang below it.

2Photograph & upload

Photograph the filled sheet flat, in good light, then upload it. Tap the centre of each black corner square in order: top-left → top-right → bottom-right → bottom-left. Upload as many sheets as you like — later sheets add alternate samples.
